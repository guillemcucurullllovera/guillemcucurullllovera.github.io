Heimdall: systematic convexity strategy platform

architecture + requirementsspec-heavy / in developmentmanual execution

A systematic options platform for The Suzie Q funded-convexity program. No component emits an order; every trade is manually entered by the principal.

Strategy

A carry-funded convexity design: index put-spread carry helps finance deep out-of-the-money long-dated tail hedges. The system is judged on growth and drawdown together, not raw return alone.

Gate

Deterministic and credit-led: credit spreads, index structure and implied volatility drive the risk state. No model call sits in the live money path.

Architecture

Separation between the deterministic money path and any advisory or research layer. Broker links, if present, are read-only. The architecture is designed for auditability and conservative governance.

Status

Presented as strategy and platform specification with server-side code diverging from later requirements; not represented as a finished, proven or autonomous hedge-fund engine.
